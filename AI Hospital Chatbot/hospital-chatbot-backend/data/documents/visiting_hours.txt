Intensive Care Unit (ICU) Visiting Hours:
- Morning: 10:00 AM to 11:00 AM
- Evening: 4:00 PM to 5:00 PM
- Maximum 2 visitors at a time. Children under 12 are not allowed in the ICU.

General Ward Visiting Hours:
- Morning: 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM
- Evening: 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM
- Visitors are requested to keep noise levels low.

Emergency Department:
- One family member can accompany the patient at all times.
- Open 24/7.
